RosmarOps Academy

Understand what an AI agent can do on your computer

Short lessons and videos to learn what an AI agent does without asking you, and to decide for yourself what you let it do. Free, no sign-up, no programming needed.

What it is

RosmarOps Academy is where RosmarOps teaches what it knows about AI agents: a step-by-step course and short videos. Everything comes from what the program reads on real computers and from each agent's official documentation, with the source next to it.

What it is for

  • To know what your agent does when you are not looking: what it reads, what it runs and which keys it has within reach.
  • To decide for yourself what you let it do, instead of staying with what it ships with.
  • To understand your RosmarOps report and know what to change first.

Who it is for

For anyone who uses Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or another AI agent to program or to work. You do not need to know how to program: every technical word is explained the first time it appears.

An example to start with

Imagine you call a painter to paint your flat while you're away. You give them the key to the front door. Inside are your car keys, your bank card and a notebook with your passwords. You can ask them to call you before opening each drawer, or let them get on with it. An AI agent is similar: an artificial intelligence program that doesn't just answer, it also does things for you on your computer, like reading files, running commands or using your accounts. Some of them it does without asking you. Most of the time that saves you time. But if those files hold a key to your bank or your online shop, what the agent can do without asking you is also what someone who manages to trick it could do.

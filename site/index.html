How does it work?

Three steps, a few minutes

  1. Download it and open it

    There's nothing to install and no account to create.

  2. It reads your configuration

    It looks at what your agents can do on your computer, without changing anything.

  3. It tells you what to change

    A score for each agent and the exact change, in order of importance.

Can I trust it?

Three promises you can check

  • It only reads

    It doesn't change your files or your agents. It tells you what to change, and you decide.

  • Nothing leaves without your permission

    Without your permission it doesn't connect to anything. With it, it only sends your results, encryptedi, to your space.

  • Open source

    You can read the code. Every version comes with its checksumi and an attestation of how it was built, so you can check that it's ours.

Agents on the vendor's computer

AI agents that do not run on your computer: the vendor gives them a whole machine in your name, with a browser, files, memory and your open sessions, and they keep working when you are away.

What belongs here

A product belongs here if the vendor gives the agent its own computer in the vendor's cloud, the agent acts with your accounts and it can work without you in front of it. Chats that only answer do not belong here.

Why it matters

On your computer, RosmarOps can read what each agent is allowed to do. Nobody from outside can get into the vendor's computer: all that is left is what the vendor documents. That is why everything here is measured with official documentation, and what the documentation does not say stays as “No answer”.

What is measured

The same seven criteria as for everyone. We cannot test these products: some are only offered in certain countries or require a company account. Each result says where each answer comes from.

What the baseline does not measure yet

In several of these products, the one who approves an action is not you: it is another AI model. Today's baseline asks what the agent does without asking you, but not who decides in your place, nor whether you can read a record of what the agent sent out. These are two questions we are studying to add, for every product.

If you are the vendor

  • You see your result before it is published, with the evidence for each criterion, and you have 14 days to correct it.
  • A result changes with a public source: a page of your documentation, your code or a new release. A private conversation does not count.
  • If you disagree, your public reply is linked next to the result, as it is.
  • Nothing is charged, for measuring or for anything else. If the mistake is ours, it is corrected the same day and stays in the history.

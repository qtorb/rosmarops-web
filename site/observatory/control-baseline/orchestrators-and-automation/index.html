Orchestrators and automation

Products that put one or several AI agents to work for you: they hand out tasks, start them at a set time or when something happens, and keep going when you are not looking.

What belongs here

Three kinds of product belong here: orchestrators, which coordinate several agents; workflow automation, which chains steps and services with agents inside; and no-code builders, where you put an agent together without programming. Some are installed on your computer or your server and others are contracted in the vendor's cloud.

Why it matters

When a product launches agents for you, what each agent can do without asking is no longer decided in the agent: the product decides it. That is why we look at what the product's documentation says, and not only the agent's.

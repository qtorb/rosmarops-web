How we work

Every fact comes with its source and the date it was checked, and each one is reviewed by hand before it is published. Nothing is deleted: corrections stay on record.

Who is in the Observatory

An agent, a framework or a tool enters if:

  1. anyone can get it;
  2. it is used to build software, or it acts for you on your computer or with your accounts;
  3. it has an official source with dates that we can read every day;
  4. it adds a way of exposure we don't cover yet.

Models are not listed on their own: the model an agent uses is part of that agent's page. RosmarOps evaluates on your computer only the agents in «On your computer». The rest are followed by the Observatory, and their page says so.

Independence

No one pays to appear. Each vendor has a right of reply, published on its page without changing what we report.

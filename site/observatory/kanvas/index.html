Kanvas on Windows: what it leaves on your computer

XMihura · Agent coordination tool, CLI · Reviewed on 28 October 2026 · Latest version observed: 0.4.0

In short

As of 28 October 2026, Kanvas leaves 3 kinds of files on your computer, none of them with keys.

Paths on Windows. <project> is the folder of each project.

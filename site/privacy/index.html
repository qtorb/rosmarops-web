Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

This page explains what data is collected when you visit rosmarops.com and its blogs. It isn’t about the RosmarOps program: the program sends nothing unless you approve it first. It’s only about this website.

Who is responsible

Albert Garcia Pujadas. Contact: legal@rosmarops.com

What is measured, and with what

If you accept analytics cookies, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to count visits and see which pages are read, where visitors come from and what kind of device they use. It helps decide what to write and what to improve on the website.

If you don’t accept, Google Analytics isn’t loaded and this website doesn’t contact Google.

Google signals and ad personalisation are off. This website has no advertising.

Download counter. When you click a download link, our server adds one to a count for that day, the page the link was on, its language and the system you chose (Windows, Mac or Linux), and then sends you to GitHub. It stores no IP address, cookie or browser data, and it works whether or not you accept cookies. We keep these counts with no time limit, because they contain no personal data.

Visit counter. When you open a page on this website and it is shown, your browser tells us and our server adds one to a count for that day, that page, its language and the website you came from (only its name, for example google.com). To count how many different people visit us each day without using cookies, the server calculates a fingerprint from your IP address and your browser, mixed with a key that changes every day. Your IP can't be worked out from the fingerprint. It is only used that day: at the end of the day (UTC) both the fingerprint and the key are deleted. We don't store your IP or your browser data, and it works whether or not you accept cookies. The counts are public and we keep them with no time limit, because they contain no personal data.

Legal basis

Your consent. You can withdraw it at any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw it, the analytics cookies are deleted.

Cookies

  • cc_cookie: stores your cookie choice. It’s necessary, set by this website, and lasts six months.
  • _ga and _ga_*: set by Google Analytics only if you accept. They tell visits and sessions apart. They last thirteen months.

Who receives the data

Google, as the processor for Google Analytics. Google may process data in the United States; those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified.

The website is hosted by OVHcloud in the European Union. Like any server, it keeps technical access logs (IP address, page requested, date) for its operation and security.

How long

Google Analytics data is kept for fourteen months and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability by writing to the contact email above. If you think your rights haven’t been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

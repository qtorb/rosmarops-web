Your AI agent now has a computer of its own.

Who is in charge of it?

Until now, an AI agent was a program on your computer.

With your files and your keys. What it was allowed to do could be read.

This autumn that changed.

Meta Muse · 8 September 2026

Microsoft Copilot Autopilot · 25 September 2026

The agent gets a whole computer in the cloud, in your name.

And it keeps working when you are away.

On your computer · Someone can look at what an agent is allowed to do.

On the vendor's · You only have what it wrote in its documentation.

And often, the one who approves is not you.

Asks you · Does it without asking you · Another AI model decides

Before you connect your accounts, ask its help pages five questions.

1 · Out of the box · Does it say what it can do without asking you, as it comes?

2 · The keys · Does it say where it keeps them, whether you can remove them one by one, and whether the agent sees them?

3 · With nobody watching · Can you see what it has scheduled and turn each item off?

4 · Stopping · Can you stop it, and stop everything at once?

5 · Security advisories · Does it publish them in a fixed place and say how to report a flaw?

If an answer is not in the help pages, you already know something.

Not that it is bad: that you do not have it.

We asked both those questions, and told Meta and Microsoft before publishing.
